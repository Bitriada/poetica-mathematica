# Provenance & Correspondence

This folder contains a formal research dossier investigating structural overlaps and chronological correspondences between the Bitriada / Fare Algebra framework and recent AI-generated mathematical proofs.

The purpose of these documents is not to allege misconduct, but to conduct a rigorous, technical inquiry into the conceptual pathways of AI discovery. They explore the hypothesis of "multiple discovery," the role of human prompt steering, latent prior-art activation, and the technical possibility of inference-time retrieval (RAG).

## The Inquiry Briefs

*   **[Poetica_Mathematica_Provenance_Inquiry_Case.pdf](Poetica_Mathematica_Provenance_Inquiry_Case.pdf)**
    *The Initial Brief.* A formal inquiry brief regarding structural correspondences between the Fare Algebra (Chapter IV, §4.1.1) and a later mathematical proof released by OpenAI. This document outlines the initial chronology, the exact technical isomorphism, and questions for provenance review.

*   **[Poetica_Mathematica_Provenance_Continuation_Multiple_Discovery_and_Retrieval.pdf](Poetica_Mathematica_Provenance_Continuation_Multiple_Discovery_and_Retrieval.pdf)**
    *The Extended Memorandum.* A deeper investigation into the 153-day window between the public Zenodo publication (1 March 2026) and OpenAI's release (1 August 2026). This document applies Robert K. Merton's historical "multiple discovery" model to AI workflows, analyzes the distinct causal pathways (human prompt design, prior literature, live retrieval), and outlines the specific, redacted evidence requests that would resolve the pathways.
