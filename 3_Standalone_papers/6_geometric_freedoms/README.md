# Generative Construction of 6 Geometric Freedoms

**A combinatorial thought experiment.**

*Can a single 0D dot build the complex plane without ever importing an outside rule?*

## The Rules of the Game

We begin with a minimal geometric operand—a "dot"—and a strict rule set:
1. **Informatic:** A single bit with two states `(0; 1)`.
2. **Geometric:** A point that serves as its own coordinates.
3. **Algebraic:** A minimal moveset of exactly two operations: `+1` (flag up) and `-1` (flag down).

**The Goal:** Reach the complex plane. 
**The Constraint:** The system must grow through incremental algebraic development. Every new operation must be reflected in its geometry and informatics. You cannot cheat by importing external concepts, coordinate spaces, or new rules. 

## What This Paper Explores

As the dot is forced to climb the ladder of number systems (Natural → Integer → Float), the algebra demands new operations. The geometry must adapt to survive. 

This paper is a schema design problem. It treats the dot as a compressed representation of three interfaces (Algebraic, Geometric, Informatic) that must remain coherent as the environment expands. It reveals how algebraic constraints cast a "shadow" into geometric form—and what happens when the system inevitably tries to operate on its own existence.
