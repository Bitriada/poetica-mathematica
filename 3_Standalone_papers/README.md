# Standalone Papers & Provenance

This folder contains primarily offprints and excerpts from the larger *Poetica Mathematica* monograph, as well as a research into the provenance of AI-generated mathematics.

The standalone papers are designed to be read independently in 3–5 minutes. They adopt an investigative, "what if" tone—starting with a simple mathematical concept and following the structural logic to its inevitable, sometimes surprising, conclusion. Each paper contains an embedded FAQ to address immediate structural objections.

# Available Papers

### [A Generative Integer Fractal from Minimal Rules](Generative_Integer_Fractal/README.md)
**Premise:** What happens if you take a single seed (2) and force it to grow using only three rules, with one strict limitation: *you cannot repeat the same operation more than three times?* 
**The Payoff:** The construction hits a wall at step 9. To escape without breaking the rules, it must execute a "grammatical turn"—a structural least-action move that reveals the 39-point lattice was actually a perfectly balanced 117-point hyper-structure all along.
*(More standalone papers will be added here as they are developed).*

**[History of Math as a Sequence of Cognitive Revolutions](./History_of_Math_as_a_Sequence_of_Cognitive_Revolutions.docx)** 
  *What toll did we pay to count differently?* A standalone thought experiment tracing the evolution of number systems from primitive tally marks to the complex plane, revealing the hidden "fare" of certainty paid at each mathematical boundary.
