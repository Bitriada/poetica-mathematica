# Tribit Face

**A tribit and its face as a Fare–Riemann sphere.**
Python + HTML, zero dependencies. Part of the Poetica Mathematica toy line
(after BCT-1/BCT-2, the color tensor, and the pi-clock).

---

## 1 · The pose

The monograph's Chapter V states the kernel of the whole framework in one
line:

> "The Tribit is the machine; the Algebroid is its language; the
> Dimensoid is its stage."

This project takes the sentence literally and makes it runnable:

| Triad facet | Monograph meaning | In this project |
|---|---|---|
| **Algebroid** | the family of algebras with their trace-tracking rule | `python/` — the UC arithmetic, the C₈ rotations, the gates, the Fare Algebra, the spectral involution; 43 checks |
| **Dimensoid** | the geometric sphere on which the discrete lattice lives | `html/` — the Fare–Riemann sphere S³_FR drawn live: Hopf fibers, balance torus, nadir circle with square-free labels |
| **Tribit** | the computational architecture that navigates them | the interactive panel — one point of the sphere, eight rotations, two gates |

A tribit is an element of the ultra-complex algebra
UC = C[τ]/(τ²):

    z = a + b·i + τ(c + d·i),    i² = −1,  τ² = 0,  iτ = τi

The **value** v = a + bi carries the classical content of a computation;
the **trace** t = c + di is a nilpotent memory recording its fare — the
receipt. Because τ² = 0 the trace cannot be duplicated or stacked: you can
remember, but only once. The Fare–Riemann sphere is the set
S³_FR = {z : |val|² + |tr|² = 1} — the round 3-sphere in R⁴ — and it is
the tribit's *geometric face*: the stage on which value, trace, rotations
and gates all become picture.

## 2 · Research background (with monograph sources)

**The sphere and why it is round.** Def. 4.2.4.1 (Ch. IV) defines S³_FR
with the Fare–Riemann norm ||z||²_FR = |val|² + |tr|². Remark 4.2.4.1a
argues the round metric is *forced*: at the spectral critical value s = ½
the involution is the identity on the trace plane, so value and trace
stand on equal footing and the symmetry group must contain the O_k
rotations. The **nadir** N = {τs : |s| = 1} (Def. 4.2.4.4) is the circle
of pure-transit states (zero value, full trace), which the monograph
reads as Turing tape configurations. The **intrinsic metric**
||z||_τ = |val| + |tr| (Def. 4.2.4.6) gives the sphere intrinsic radius
√2 (Prop. 4.2.4.7).

**The two fibrations.** Sec. 4.2.4 calls the value map a Hopf fibration
and writes fibers that fix the value: over v, the trace circle
{v + τt : |t|² = 1 − |v|²}. These are the circles the C₈ rotations
navigate (Sec. 4.5.2a: O_k rotates the trace by kπ/4 at fixed value).
Classically, the Hopf fibration S³ → S² has fibers {(u, q·u)}; under
h(z) = tr(z)/val(z) the fiber at infinity is exactly the nadir — the
monograph's "fiber at ∞ = pure trace". The two readings are *different
fibrations*; the page draws both (amber value-fibers, gray Hopf tori)
and the check suite proves they are distinct (S-7). This tension is
flagged, not hidden.

**The fare ladder.** Sec. 4.4.7–4.4.8: the transit algebras nest
T₃ ⊂ UC ⊂ T (TFA, generated by i, τ, θ with i² = −1, τ² = 0, θ³ = 8θ),
and the nesting is realized geometrically as S¹ ⊂ S³ ⊂ S⁷ — "each level
of the Bitriada tower is a sphere embedded in the next". This project
lives on the middle rung, the 3-sphere of UC; the lower rung is the
trace circle, the upper rung (TFA, the 7-sphere) is the next project.

**AGS.** Sec. 4.5: Tribit Space is the Transit–Fare Algebra viewed as the
unified stage on which the Algebraic, Geometric and Spectral axes
operate simultaneously — the what (Fare Algebra F), the where (UC), the
how (TFA), organized by the AGS triplet (θ-grade, i-grade, τ-grade).
The sphere is where the three perspectives synchronize.

**The idea behind it: the H-prime Algebroid–Dimensoid–Tribit union.**
Ch. V §5.1: the Epoch-5 structures (F, UC, TFA) are the H-level
(exponentiation-level) prime-zone structures of the 1DB lattice, and at
every convergent DB three facets unite — the Algebroid (algebras +
nilpotent Leibniz trace rule, the Fare Principle), the Dimensoid (a
sphere built by a triadic rule: two proper spheres as skeleton, one
ghost disc as binding surface, τ² = 0 realized as "the ghost disc
contributes no volume"; for the extrinsic Epoch-5 lattices the
dimensoid *is* the Fare–Riemann sphere, with the discrete
square-free-labelled lattice placed on it by the spectral involution),
and the Tribit (the machine: UC point, R⁴ frame with three projections
π_val, π_geom, π_info, the C₈ group, the gates 0/a and ω/0). The
dimensoid also has an inner reading (§2.7.2): four nested folds —
point, line, plane, sphere — joined by three null transits, which is
exactly the four coordinates (a, b, c, d) of the tribit; its three ways
of counting (4, 16, 64) match End(C), End(H), End(O).

**The Fare Algebra.** Def. 4.1.1.1: F = C[τ_p : p prime]/(τ_p²), basis
τ_n over square-free n, multiplication τ_m·τ_n = τ_{mn} when gcd = 1 and
0 otherwise. The spectral involution ι_s(τ_n) = n^{1−2s}τ_n
(Def. 4.1.1.4) encodes the zeta functional equation: ι_s∘ι_{1−s} = id
(Claim 4.1.1.5) and Z(s) = ι_s(Z(1−s)) (Claim 4.1.1.6). The collapse
φ_UC : F → UC sends every τ_n (n > 1) to the single τ — "retaining only
the binary fact that a prime was present" (Sec. 4.1.5).

**The gates.** Sec. 4.5.2a: atlas 0/a kills the trace (classical
readout); overdrive ω/0 lifts the value into the trace. The pair is a
one-way switch: 0/a∘ω/0 = id on values, the reverse loses the trace;
overdrive is idempotent because τ² = 0. A pleasant identity verified
here: **overdrive is multiplication by τ**, z·τ = τ·val(z).

## 4 · The face: reading the picture

The canvas is a stereographic projection of S³_FR from the pure-value
pole, orbitable by drag, zoomable by wheel or pinch.

- **amber ring** — the value fiber over your tribit's value: the circle
  the O_k rotations navigate. Set the trace angle dial and watch the
  point glide along it.
- **gray nested tori** — a ring of classical Hopf fibers h⁻¹(q),
  q = r·e^{iα}: the other reading of the sphere.
- **violet grid** — the balance torus |val| = |tr| = 1/√2 (the Clifford
  torus): the locus of the AGS balance condition at s = ½, and the set
  where the intrinsic norm attains its supremum √2.
- **green circle** — the nadir: pure transit, zero value. The small
  green numbers are square-free labels placed by logarithmic height
  Φ(n) = 2π·ln n/ln 211, under which coprime multiplication is exactly
  angle addition: Φ(mn) = Φ(m) + Φ(n).
- **cyan dot** — the tribit itself, always shown normalized to the
  sphere (the panel reports the raw coordinates).

Panel controls: the four coordinate sliders; the buttons O₁…O₇
(animated 45° steps); the continuous trace-angle dial; the gates
0/a (atlas) and ω/0 (overdrive); multiplication by τ; multiplication by
an arbitrary tribit w (the Leibniz rule in action); snap-to-sphere;
reset. The badge at the top re-runs the 35 Python golden vectors
through the page's own JavaScript math core on every load.

## 6 · Honest limits

1. **The Hopf tension.** The monograph's Sec. 4.2.4 names the value map
   a Hopf fibration while its explicit fibers fix the value; the
   classical Hopf fibers are a different family. Both are implemented
   and drawn; check S-7 proves the difference. We do not resolve which
   reading the framework ultimately wants.
2. **Prop. 4.2.3.5 vs Def. 4.2.3.3.** The proposition's 4×4 matrix has
   two identical rotation blocks (value rotates too); the definition
   and the TriPU description leave the value plane fixed. We implement
   the definition as primary (`O_matrix`) and the two-block form as the
   TFA-lifted grammar (`O_matrix_lifted`, Remark 4.2.3.3a).
3. **The collapse is not a homomorphism.** φ(τ₂·τ₃) = φ(τ₆) = τ while
   φ(τ₂)·φ(τ₃) = τ² = 0. The monograph's own wording ("linear
   surjections", Sec. 4.5.2a) is correct; a ring-homomorphism reading
   would be false. The collapse records *presence*, not arithmetic.
4. **Square-free label angles are our choice.** The monograph assigns
   "an angle φ_p to each prime" without pinning the assignment. We use
   the logarithmic height, which makes coprimality multiplication exact
   angle addition and is injective on n ≤ 210. The full 117-point
   lattice (Chapter I construction) is not reproduced.
5. **The Clifford identification is ours.** That the AGS balance locus
   |val| = |tr| on the sphere is the Clifford torus is a classical
   fact we verify numerically (S-11); the monograph does not name it.
6. **The ladder's other rungs are named, not implemented.** T₃, T₆ and
   the TFA (θ³ = 8θ) appear in `fare.py` docstrings and the README
   only. They are the next rung.
7. **What verification means here.** The monograph marks many of its
   statements as *Claim* — the author's proposals. This project
   verifies their internal consistency and their agreement with
   classical mathematics (nilpotent algebras, dual numbers, the Hopf
   fibration, Cauchy–Schwarz). It does not verify the framework's
   physics, its zeta conjectures, or the Epoch narrative.
